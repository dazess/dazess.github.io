# Asset provenance

## AwardScience

`awardscience.webp` is a screenshot extracted from the user-supplied public product demo: <https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NXfa7q_kYhk>, “Build an entire award campaign with AI — in minutes, not weeks”, by AwardScience. It is shown to document a product the portfolio owner states they helped build. The exact frame time is recorded in the image metadata. The original video is not shipped with the site.

## WhatsApp Health Bot

`healthbot-dashboard.webp` and `healthbot-detail.webp` are browser screenshots of the original Jinja templates in `whatsapp-health-bot/templates/index.html` and `patient_detail.html`, rendered offline with fictional fixtures by `.design-tools/render-healthbot.py`. The screenshots carry a fictional-data banner. No live database, authentication session, or messaging backend was used.

## Typography and vectors

Manrope: Google Fonts / Mikhail Sharanda, licensed under SIL Open Font License 1.1. Self-hosted Latin variable font. Icons, the asterisk, simple apple geometry, and favicon were authored as SVG for this portfolio; they are not stock screenshots or AI-generated photographs.

## Fruit Box demo

Game concept and English-translated rules are inspired by [Fruit Box by Game Saien](https://www.gamesaien.com/game/fruit_box_a/) and its [Japanese instruction page](https://www.gamesaien.com/game/fruit_box_a/guide/). The reference states: draw a rectangle around apples totaling 10; separated apples can be selected across gaps; the time limit is 120 seconds; each cleared apple earns one point. The portfolio's compact 6×6 implementation, pointer/touch/keyboard controls, and SVG apples were authored independently. No original scripts, sprites, advertising, or analytics are included.
